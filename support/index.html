Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Settings tab in Masar, then Account and purchases → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Restoring needs an internet connection.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Masar. You can also get there from the app: Settings → Account and purchases → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What is the Exam Pass?

The Exam Pass is a one-time purchase that gives you full access for 60 days. It does not renew and you won’t be charged again. You can see when it ends in Settings → Account and purchases.

Why can’t I see AI explanations?

AI explanations use Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on. Apple Intelligence isn’t available in every language or region, and in some languages (such as Arabic, Hindi and Urdu) the AI explanations appear in English. Masar works fully without it: standard explanations are always there. Transcribing your speaking answers also needs a recent iOS version.

Does Masar work offline?

Yes. Lessons, practice and mock exams all work without an internet connection. You only need a connection to buy or restore a plan.

How do I back up or move my progress?

Go to Settings → Data and privacy → Export progress and save the backup file wherever you like (for example in Files). On another iPhone, use Settings → Data and privacy → Import progress and pick that file. Importing replaces the progress on that iPhone. Your progress is normally also included in your iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone.

How do I delete my data?

  • Speaking recordings: Settings → Data and privacy → Offline and storage → Delete recordings.
  • Everything: Settings → Data and privacy → Delete all my data. This can’t be undone.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.

We don’t store any of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. See the Privacy Policy.

Is my practice score an official IELTS band?

No. Scores and estimates in Masar are for learning only and aren’t official IELTS results.

Is Masar connected to IELTS?

No. Masar is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the British Council, IDP IELTS or Cambridge University Press & Assessment. IELTS is their trademark.
