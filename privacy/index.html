Privacy Policy

Masar – Learn IELTS (iPhone app) · Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how the Masar app (“Masar”, “the app”) handles your information. “We” and “us” mean the developer of Masar.

In short

  • Masar has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect any personal data. Masar has no server of its own, and your learning data is never sent to us.
  • There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone, and you can delete it at any time.

Information stored on your iPhone

To work, the app saves the following in its own local storage on your device:

  • your learning progress, answers, review schedule and achievements;
  • your study settings and preferences, such as your target band, exam date, reminder time and interface language, plus anything optional you choose to add, such as a name or an official result;
  • your writing drafts;
  • your speaking recordings and their transcripts;
  • explanations generated on the device by Apple Intelligence (kept so they don’t have to be generated again).

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, access it or recover it for you.

No developer server, no network use for your data

All lessons, questions and audio are included in the app, and Masar works offline. The app makes no network requests of its own to send or receive your data. An internet connection is only used by Apple’s own system services, for example to buy or restore a purchase through the App Store, or when you open a link (such as this page) in Safari.

Apple Intelligence and speech transcription

Optional AI explanations use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence), and optional transcription of your spoken answers uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition. Both run on your iPhone; Masar does not send your answers, recordings or transcripts to us or to any AI service. These features are only available on supported iPhones. iOS may download language models or speech assets from Apple to make them work; that download is handled by iOS, and Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to it.

Microphone and speech recognition

Masar asks for microphone access only for speaking practice, and it records only while you are recording an answer. Recordings are saved on your iPhone. If you allow speech recognition, Masar uses it to transcribe your practice answers on the device so you can review them. You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Notifications

Daily study reminders are optional. They are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone by the app; Masar does not use push notifications or a notification server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in the iPhone Settings app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the Exam Pass are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information. The app checks what you have bought using Apple’s StoreKit on your device. Apple’s terms and Privacy Policy apply to purchases.

Like all App Store developers, we can see aggregated sales and App Store statistics from Apple, and crash reports only from people who have chosen to share them with app developers in iOS Settings. This information does not identify you to us.

Backups, export and import

  • Device backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app’s data may be included as part of your own backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.
  • Export. You can export a backup file of your progress in the app. It opens the iOS share sheet, and you choose where the file goes (for example Files, AirDrop or email). The file leaves your iPhone only if you share it.
  • Import. You can import a Masar backup file that you pick. It replaces the progress on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your speaking recordings: Settings → Data and privacy → Offline and storage → Delete recordings.
  • Delete everything (progress, recordings and scheduled reminders): Settings → Data and privacy → Delete all my data. This can’t be undone.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes all of its data from the device. Copies in your own backups or exported files are under your control.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, we don’t share it, and we delete it when it is no longer needed to help you.

Children

Masar is suitable for general audiences. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You are in full control of the data on your iPhone through the options above. If you have any questions about privacy, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s handling of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
